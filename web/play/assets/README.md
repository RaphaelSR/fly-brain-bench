# Courtyard image sources

The six JPEG maps are local, resized/recompressed 1K copies of Poly Haven assets,
licensed CC0: https://polyhaven.com/license . No runtime requests go to Poly Haven.

- Terracotta Floor Tiles, Dimitrios Savva: https://polyhaven.com/a/terracotta_floor_tiles
- Painted Plaster Wall: https://polyhaven.com/a/painted_plaster_wall

Each material includes diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness maps. Download source:
`https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/jpg/1k/ASSET/ASSET_MAP_1k.jpg`
where ASSET is the asset slug and MAP is `diff`, `nor_gl` or `rough`.
JPEGs recompressed at quality 72, at most 1024 px. These are visual materials,
not evidence for the fly's biology or calibrated physical units.
